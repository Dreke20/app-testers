How testing works

Use the same Google account that is signed in on your phone’s Play Store for every step.

01

Join that app’s Google Group

Join with the same Google account as your Play Store. If a group is still awaiting Google approval, that card will say “Opening soon” and the links will stay locked.

02

Become a tester

Open the official Google Play opt-in page and tap Become a tester.

03

Install from Play

Wait a few minutes, then install the app from its Play Store link. The store page stays hidden until you have opted in.

04

Open it daily for 14 days

Stay opted in, keep the app installed, and open it for about 1 minute every day during the 14-day closed test. Daily use really helps us get the app through Google Play testing.

Google Play requires at least 12 testers to stay opted in for 14 consecutive days before a new personal developer account can apply for production. Uninstalling or leaving early can reset that clock.

    Common questions

    Why 14 days?

    Google Play requires at least 12 testers to stay opted in for 14 consecutive days before a new personal developer account can apply for production. Uninstalling or leaving the test early can reset that clock.

    The Play Store link says “App not found.” What now?

    That’s normal until you join the tester group and tap Become a tester. Google only shows the store page to people who have opted in. If you already opted in, make sure you’re signed into the same Google account on this page and in the Play Store, wait a few minutes, then try the Install link again.

    Which Google account should I use?

    Use the same Google account for the Google Group, the Become a tester page, and the Play Store on your phone. A different account is the most common reason the install page doesn’t appear.

    How do I leave a test?

    Open the same Become a tester page for that app and choose to leave the test. You can also uninstall the app, and leave the Google Group if you want. Leaving early can reset the 14-day tester clock for that app.

    Is it free?

    Yes. Joining a closed test and installing the app is free.

    What phone do I need?

    Android only, through Google Play. These tests aren’t available on iPhone.

    Can I list my own app here?

    Yes, if you are an indie Android developer in Google Play closed testing. The first 20 apps are free, then $29 per app. We recruit real testers and give you a card, tracker, daily-open reminders, and a weekly feedback report. We don’t guarantee a tester count or Google’s approval, and we never pay for installs or reviews. Use the List your app form above, or email support.

    Can you build an app for my business?

    Yes. Use the Request an app form above. Builds are Android-first. iOS can be quoted on request. Prices on this page are starting-at estimates. A free consult comes first, then a written quote. I don’t guarantee Google Play approval or a download count.

    Will the tester counts update when I join?

    The public count on each card updates when someone taps I’ve joined & opted in and sends the Google account they used to join the group. That is a self-report. This site cannot read Google Play’s official opted-in list, so a check-in here is not proof that Play has counted you. If the live count can’t be loaded, the card falls back to the last number we entered by hand.

    What data does this page collect?

    This page doesn’t collect anything unless you send a form or check in as a tester. The feedback form asks which app you mean, a description, and optionally your email, device, and app version so we can follow up. The I’ve joined & opted in form asks for the Google account email you used to join the tester group, and optionally a first name for the thank-you wall. The listing form asks for your name, email, app name, Play link or package, how many testers you have, and a message. The build request form asks for your name, email, business or app name, app idea, platform, budget range, timeline, optional management package, and notes. Submissions go to our support inbox and a private list. We don’t sell this information. Opt-in emails are never shown on this page. Each app also has its own privacy policy, linked on its card and in the footer.
