Privacy

This page covers the App Lab website at apps.dirtysouthagency.com, run by Dirty South Marketing Agency in Macon, Georgia. It does not cover the Android apps themselves. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked on its card and in the footer.

We don’t collect anything from you unless you send a form or check in as a tester. We don’t sell this information. Testers must be 18 or older.

What this site collects

Feedback form. Which app, the type (Bug, Idea, or Other), a description of what happened, and optionally your email, device / Android version, and app version.

I’ve joined & opted in (check-in). The Google account email you used to join the tester group, the app you checked in for, and optionally a first name for the thank-you wall. Your email is stored privately and is never shown on this page. If you give a first name, it may appear on the public thank-you wall.

Listing request form. Your name, email, app name, and optionally a Play Store link or package name, how many testers you have now, and a message.

Build request form. Your name, email, business or app name, app idea, platform, budget range, timeline, and optionally a management package and notes.

Where it’s stored

Form and check-in submissions are sent to a Google Apps Script web app and stored in Google Sheets. If that script can’t be reached, the site may open an email to dirtysouthmarketingagency@gmail.com with the same details.

The how-to-join checklist on each app card stays on this device only, in your browser’s local storage. After a check-in, the site also stores that Google account email in local storage on this device so the card can remember you checked in. The 18+ box is only used on the page to unlock the sign-up buttons. It is not sent to us.

Third parties

This site loads or links to:

  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces on the pages.
  • Google Apps Script, to receive forms and check-ins and to load the public tester counts and thank-you names.
  • jsDelivr, which serves the three.js library used for the homepage background animation.
  • Links you can choose to follow: the App Lab tester Google Group, Google Play opt-in and store pages, Whop (for the $29 listing payment after we confirm a listing), Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, the app feature overviews, and each app’s own privacy policy.

We did not find any analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts on this site. If you pay on Whop, or use Google Group or Google Play, those services have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep form and check-in records until you ask us to delete them, and we delete them when you ask. Email dirtysouthmarketingagency@gmail.com.

How to ask us to delete your information

Email dirtysouthmarketingagency@gmail.com and tell us what you want removed. You can also call (478) 663-9259. We’ll delete your form and check-in records from our Google Sheet, and we’ll take your name off the thank-you wall if it’s there.
