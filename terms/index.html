Terms of use

Dirty South’s App Lab is run by Dirty South Marketing Agency in Macon, Georgia. These terms cover this website, apps.dirtysouthagency.com, and the App Lab listing offer.

Testers

Joining a closed test and installing an app from App Lab is free. Testers must be 18 or older. The site keeps the sign-up buttons locked until you tick the 18+ box on an app card.

We do not guarantee a tester count or Google Play approval. We never pay for installs, ratings, or Play Store reviews.

Listing an app

If you want to list an app on App Lab, send a listing request first so we can confirm it is a fit. Founding spots are free (the first 20 apps). After that, the App Lab Listing is a one-time $29 fee, paid on Whop after we confirm the listing.

A listing does not guarantee a tester count or Google’s approval. We never pay for installs or reviews.

Refunds

If you want to ask about a refund, email dirtysouthmarketingagency@gmail.com.

Requesting a custom app

The “Request an app” form is a request for a consult, not a paid project. Submitting it does not start billed work. A written quote comes later if we take the work on.
